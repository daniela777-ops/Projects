Nutrition Tips

Eating well is a big part of staying healthy and balanced. Here are a few basic tips to support your wellness journey:

  • 💧 Stay hydrated — aim for 6–8 glasses of water daily
  • 🥦 Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables
  • 🍞 Choose whole grains over refined ones
  • 🧡 Don't skip meals — especially breakfast
  • 🍫 Enjoy treats in moderation (you deserve it!)

Remember, nutrition isn’t about perfection — it’s about balance, kindness, and listening to your body. 💕
