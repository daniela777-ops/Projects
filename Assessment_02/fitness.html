Fitness & Movement

Physical activity doesn’t have to be intense to be effective! Moving your body regularly can boost your energy, improve mood, and support your overall health.

  • 🚶‍♀️ Take short walks during the day
  • 🧘‍♀️ Try stretching or light yoga to relax
  • 🎶 Dance to your favorite music — it counts!
  • 💪 Start with small home workouts (no gym needed)
  • ⏳ Even 10 minutes a day makes a difference

Find what feels good and make it fun — your body deserves to move in ways that feel joyful! ✨
